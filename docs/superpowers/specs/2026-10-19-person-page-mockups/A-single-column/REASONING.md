# A — a single-column page under the Friends tab

**Style.** Library's frame: the Social header, the tab strip with Friends active, a back row, then the person in the 1280 container. The kit's type ramp, glass tiers and Settings card; nothing drawn that the app does not draw today.

**Decisions.**
- Their half first: the head (tile at 48, the name, the key and provenance at the edge), the acts strip with every act, the act rows with every act. Both are the opened grid card's pieces, given room.
- Your half after, as one Settings card, *Your view of Nick*: Name as a text row, Show their picture and Hide as switch rows with descriptions, Colour as a stacked field over the swatches and the ring, the foot with the key and Remove friend. Each row is a kit row that already exists.
- The reader's page (`you.html`) is the same page with the acts alone and one pointer line to Settings → Social, where the published profile already lives.
- The route is the address: the rail's press, the grid card's press and the browser's back all land here.

**Requirements.**
- A page per person: yes, one route, one column.
- Override display details: the whole card, bounded and labelled as yours.
- Reader's own page: yes, acts only, no second place to edit the profile.
- Members followed through a relay: the same page, *on <host>* in the head, no Remove friend.

**Trade-offs.**
- Gains: no grid reflow, a stable address, one nav zone per region in reading order, works on a TV as a long scroll.
- Sacrifices: the settings are below the fold on a person with many acts; the reader scrolls past the activity to reach Hide or Remove.
