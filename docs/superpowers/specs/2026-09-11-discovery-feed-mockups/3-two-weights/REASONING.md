# 3 · Two weights

**Style.** Two entry sizes for two kinds of act. A recommendation is a full entry on `.glass`: a person-first header (22px `.avatar` initial, name, "recommended", sentiment glyph, "· 2h ago"), the note as a body line, then the title as an embedded `.inset` card with the 48×72 thumb, name, meta, markers and the mast on the card's right edge. A listing is a compact entry on `.inset`: one line, no avatar, a 28×42 thumb, "Cleo wants to watch **Sample Show** · 12m ago" with meta and markers trailing at 55%, the mast on the entry's right edge. Consecutive compact entries sit 5px apart instead of 10px, so a run of listings reads as one quiet block between the things people said.

**Decisions.**

* The compact entry is the full entry's card with the header folded into its first line. Same surface (`.inset`), same thumb gradient, same `.mast` overhang, same "Ignore" position. Two weights of one part, not two parts.
* The note is the body of the full entry, in the person's voice at 88% white. The overview, when there is no note (entry 6), goes *inside the card* at 55% as `.id .s` — the title's voice, not the person's. An entry without a note therefore is lighter by exactly the missing line; nothing is padded in to keep the height.
* The sentiment glyph sits in the header after the verb (♥ in the love rose, 👍 for like), matching the pennant vocabulary, so the header and the mast agree without a second symbol set.
* Hover is a surface lift only: `.glass` .045 → .065, `.inset` .035 → .055. "Ignore" is opacity-revealed text with `margin-left:auto`, so on both weights it lands at the end of the first line, before the mast. The whole entry is the click target.
* The mast follows the brief's data. Entry 1's mast is Nick 👍 only — the brief omits Cleo's own bookmark on Cleo's own listing — so the rule I applied is: a listing entry does not repeat the act its line already states. Entries 5 and 8 have no other friend's act on the title and so carry no mast. Entry 7 (Show B) shows "In library" because markers are title state and entry 4 established it; the brief lists the marker on entry 4 only.

**Requirements.** Same eight acts in the brief's order; listings 1, 3, 5, 8 compact, recommendations 2, 4, 6, 7 full. Person and time on every entry in sentence voice. Entry 4 hovered with Ignore visible; the compact hovered state shown once under a "state" label after the feed, so the feed's rhythm is not interrupted by a duplicate. "Show older" as a `.btn.ghost` closes the window. Placeholder titles and the four friends only. Colour: the rose love pennant and glyph, nothing else; per-person hue only in `.avatar`. No dividers, no badges, no bars, no animation beyond the 120ms opacity on the verb.

**Trade-offs.** Gains: a scan of the page separates "what did people say" from "what do people want" without reading; the same-person-same-title case (2 then 3) reads as a statement followed by a footnote instead of a duplicate card. Costs: two templates to keep in step (the fold has to stay honest — every field the card carries must exist in the compact line); compact entries hide the person's avatar, so a person is recognised by name alone there; the mast on a 58px-tall compact entry has room for two pennants, a third would clip.

**At 3 friends.** Recommendations are rare and listings are a handful a week. The page is mostly full entries with the odd compact line between them; the weight difference reads as emphasis and the feed stays short enough to scan whole.

**At 30 friends, listings 10:1.** The page becomes runs of compact lines — eight or ten in a row at 58px each, about 500px per run — punctuated by a full entry every screen or so. This is the case the direction is built for: the runs are quiet enough not to compete, the full entries stand out as the things to read, and the 5px inter-compact gap keeps a run from reading as ten separate cards. The failure mode is a full entry drowning between two long runs; the glass border and shadow are what keep it afloat, and they must not be softened. The Watchlist tab already answers "what do people want" as a list, so if runs get long the model still has a home for them.

**Long note.** The body is a normal paragraph and wraps at the entry width (~700px), so a four-line note costs four lines and nothing else moves: header above, card below. There is no truncation. The card is not pushed off-screen because the note is bounded by what a friend typed in a text field, not by a synopsis. If notes routinely exceed five lines the answer is a line clamp with the modal carrying the rest, not a smaller card.

**Risk: two weights read as two features.** A viewer could take compact entries for a different feed folded into this one — a "watchlist ticker" under the "recommendations". Three things hold the reading together: both weights use the same sentence voice with the person first; the compact line is visibly the card's line (same thumb gradient, same meta order, same mast); and Ignore behaves the same in both. What would break it is giving the compact entries their own header, their own sort, or their own surface colour — any of those turns a weight into a section.
