# Q · Switchboard — reasoning

Not a direction: G's page with every knob of round 10 live, and the
comparison sheet beside it.

`index.html` — the panel at the bottom right sets the round's attributes on
`<html>` (`round10.css` does the rest); the presets Q1–Q6 are the direction
pages; the state is written to the address so a combination can be named
by its link, and `panel=off` in the hash hides the panel for embedding.
Knobs that the others make moot are greyed: the ink tone and the still's
extent without an ink unit, the hairline without flush units, the dissolve
under the full still.

`compare.html` — today's page (the switchboard with today's knobs and the
panel hidden, dashed) and the six presets in iframes at half the 1920
render: the 10-foot check on one screen. Each caption links to the full
page.

Why a switchboard and not more pages: the owner asked for "a variety of
options and parameters". The directions are the options; the parameters are
continuous enough (three grounds, three tones, three widths, three
dissolves) that pages for each would be a wall of near-duplicates. The
presets keep the reasoning; the knobs keep the parameters.
