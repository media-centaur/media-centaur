# S4 · Picture left, typed title — reasoning

**Knobs** picture `inset` · time `edge` · logo `none` · on Q6's page.

## Style

S1's composition with round 11's R1 instead of R2: no logo, the typed
title at 28px on line 2, the band at 224, the thumbnail 320×180. Tile,
picture, words, the time at the edge.

## Decisions

* **The composition apart from the logo.** The owner has not chosen on
  round 11; this page shows the picture's place and the time's place with
  the settled typography, so the two decisions can be made separately.
* **224 kept.** Without the logo the band does not grow, and the
  thumbnail is 180 tall — the whole frame at a size that still reads from
  the couch.

## Requirements

* *Not the image on the right, not the time in the middle* — as S1.
* *A good idea* — the smallest change to the shipped band that answers
  both complaints: the poster out, the backdrop into its slot at 16:9, the
  time's anchor moved.

## Trade-offs

Gains: the tightest band of the round; four and a half bands above the
fold at 1080; nothing new to learn. Costs: the least presence of the four;
the smallest picture. In the app: as S1 without the logo work.
