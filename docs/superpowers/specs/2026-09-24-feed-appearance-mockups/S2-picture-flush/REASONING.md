# S2 · Picture flush — reasoning

**Knobs** picture `flush` · time `edge` · logo `headline` (72) · on Q6's page.

## Style

The still is the band's left end: full height, the whole frame (469×264),
no radius, no frame. The tile follows at the picture's edge, then the
words, then the time at the right edge. The hairline between bands runs
from the picture's edge, so the column of pictures reads as a strip down
the left with a text panel beside it.

## Decisions

* **The picture as the band's end, not an object in it.** S1 frames a
  thumbnail; S2 lets the picture be the band's left third. Bigger, and
  the picture column becomes a filmstrip.
* **The tile after the picture.** With the picture flush there is no ink
  at the left to put the tile on; it moves to the picture's right and the
  words follow it. The author mark is second.
* **The whole frame**, as S1.

## Requirements

* *Not the image on the right* — the image is the left.
* *Not the time in the middle* — the time is at the edge.
* *A good idea* — the tile-and-panel composition is a standard media
  tile; the picture is larger than S1's for the same band.

## Trade-offs

Gains: the largest picture of the left-hand compositions; a strong
vertical strip at the column's edge. Costs: the author mark is no longer
first, which UIDR-038's anatomy put first and the whole campaign has kept;
a bright frame at the band's edge has no ink around it to hold it. In the
app: as S1 with the backdrop at the band's origin and the tile and body
offset by the picture's width.
