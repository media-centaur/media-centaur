live, and the comparison sheet beside it.

rounds' stylesheets do the rest. The presets S1–S4 are the direction
pages; the state is in the address; `panel=off` hides the panel for
embedding. The logo height and the mix are greyed without a logo; the
hairline without flush units.

`compare.html` — R2 (round 11's recommendation: the picture in a box on
the right, the time at the zone's edge, mid-band) as the reference,
dashed, then the four at half the 1920 render.

copying it, and adds an empty thumbnail slot to the no-artwork band for
the left-hand compositions.
