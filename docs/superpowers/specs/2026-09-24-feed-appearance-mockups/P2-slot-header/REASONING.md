# P2 · Slot header — reasoning

O's three rail cards, two tiers (matte below 3, gold at 3+). Slots at x 6 · 34 · 62: 96 = 6 + 28 + 28 + 28 + 6, so the slots touch and the glyph's inner margin is the gap. Page card: band 44, slots 11 · 51 · 91. An absent act has no element.

## The scan

Works. At half size the eye is a glyph at the centre; left is a review, right a bookmark. In rows 2 and 3 the two posters with an eye (Cleo's Pioneer One, Charade) are found by position. In row 1 the eye is legible but must be read per poster.

## Cost

On the poster: every Feed poster carries an act, so every poster gets the band — the top quarter in ink, the art faint beneath, gaining nothing. Three ink bars per card. The .7 edge visibly darkens Cosmos's plume.

Above: the poster is whole; the card is 36 taller (204 → 240). Matte and gold read as on the disc. The row reads as its poster's label; the 16 gap keeps each glyph over its own.

## Sparse

On the poster, a one-act band is two-thirds bare. A review glyph at the left reads as a heading with room; a bookmark alone at the right (Big Buck Bunny, Metropolis) reads as placed, the empty left like a missing item. Above, the question does not arise: nothing is drawn where nothing is.

## Pick

Row 3, above. It keeps the scan, leaves the poster intact (disc and band both land on faces on half the set) and costs 36 of card height, not a quarter of every poster. If the mark must be on the poster, row 2 over the disc: position carries the kind and a capsule is never needed.
