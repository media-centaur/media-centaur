# O · Metal tiers — reasoning

J's three rail cards, posters 96×144. The backing is J's in every tier — the ink disc 44 with its white/18 ring, or an ink band 36 across the poster's top — so there is no size to drift. The glyph carries the tier as its material: matte white/78 at 1, silver at 2, gold at 3+, each metal one 135° `linearGradient` fill, the glyph 28 throughout. Cleo's filled-disc card closes the page.

## Row 1 · the disc

**Gold vs the rest:** a glance at half size, the only hue on the card.

**Silver vs matte: no.** The 92→62→84 sweep averages to the matte's 78; at 14px the silver eye on Pioneer One and the matte eye on Charade are the same light-grey eye. At 1× side by side the silver shows its darker band; alone it is a white glyph. A 1px darker edge behind the fill changed nothing and was dropped. In practice: two tiers, white and gold, N's Fill moved to the glyph.

**The mixed capsule:** gold heart over matte eye on one ink backing reads as two acts with two counts, each legible. The filled version paints the eye gold with the heart.

## Row 2 · the header band

Same separations on the flattest ground possible; silver still fails. Cost: 36 of 144, 25% off the top: Pioneer One's helmet crown, Charade's figure's head, Cosmos's plume. Titles survive at the foot. Three posters become three ink bars, a header rather than a mark.

## For comparison · the filled disc

Pixel extents are identical (on Pioneer One the ink and silver discs both span x120–163), yet the gold disc on Cosmos still reads a step larger than the ink disc on the same poster: a light fill on a dark ground spreads, and a darker rim does not cancel it. Reads as a coin.

## Pick

The disc: the band's tiers with the poster's top intact, and the capsule tells two counts. Over the filled disc: less gold, no size drift, an honest capsule.

## Metal or trophy

A gold glyph is a mark, the heart everyone loved; a gold disc is a medal. Silver as a material does not register at 14px. A third tier needs value or hue, not finish: matte near 60 (the common tier dims) or the silver toward steel (chroma .04). Outside the brief; not built.
