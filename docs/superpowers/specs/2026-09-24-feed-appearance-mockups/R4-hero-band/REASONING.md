# R4 · Hero band — reasoning

**Knobs** logo `hero` · logo height 72 · mix `some` · still `full` · on Q6's page.

## Style

The Home hero's anatomy at band scale: the full still under a scrim heavy
at the left, the logo first at the top of the text zone, then who did what,
then the review. The band is 264. A title without a logo sets its name at
34px where the logo would be.

## Decisions

* **The app's one artwork idiom, carried.** Home's hero, the marquee and
  the collection rail all put the logo over the backdrop with a left scrim
  and the name as fallback; this is that, twenty times.
* **Title first.** The hero leads with the title because the hero is about
  the title. On the Feed that inverts the sentence — the band says
  "Charade / You reviewed" — and the tile, vertically centred, sits beside
  the second line rather than the first. Recorded as the cost; the page
  exists to see whether the presence is worth it.
* **Full still.** The hero's picture is the whole surface; the wide box
  would make this R2 with the lines swapped.

## Requirements

* *Backdrop and logo* — as the hero does it.
* *A good idea* — one idiom across Home and the Feed; the reader already
  knows how to read it.
* *Couch* — the words on ≥ .84 ink with the shadow; the logo at 72.

## Trade-offs

Gains: the strongest presence of the four; one idiom app-wide. Costs: the
sentence order; the tile's position relative to the author line; the
thinner slice of the full still (a 38% slice at 264); 40px per band. In the
app: as R2 with the logo above line 1 and the round-10 "full" scrim.
