# S6 · Picture left, logo on it — reasoning

**Knobs** picture `inset` · logo `picture` · time `edge` · on Q6's page.

## Style

S5's treatment on S1's thumbnail: the still framed in the poster's slot at
the tile's right (320×180, radius 8, the poster's outline and shadow), the
Home card's gradient and logo bottom left inside it, the year under. The
tile stays first; the words are the sentence and the review; the time at
the edge; the band 224.

## Decisions

* **The tile first.** The one thing S5 gives up. Here the settled order —
  author, picture, words — holds, and the picture carries the title as
  Home's card does.
* **A smaller card.** 320×180 is the frame whole at the band's height
  less the pads; the logo caps at 64, taller in proportion than Home's
  80 on 453, so a wide wordmark fills its 80%.

## Requirements

* *The logo atop the backdrop as Home does it* — yes, on a framed
  thumbnail rather than the band's edge.
* *Not the image on the right, not the time in the middle* — as S1.
* *A good idea* — the sibling of S5 that keeps the author mark where the
  campaign has kept it.

## Trade-offs

Gains: S5's idiom with the settled reading order; the framed thumbnail
sits calmly in the band. Costs: the smallest picture of the on-picture
pair; the logo is proportionally larger on it, so a loud logo is louder
here. In the app: as S5 with the picture at the poster's position and
radius.
