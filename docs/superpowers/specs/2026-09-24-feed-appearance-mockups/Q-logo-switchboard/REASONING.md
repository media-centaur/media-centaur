# Q · Logo switchboard — reasoning

Not a direction: Q6's page with the poster removed from every band and
every knob of rounds 10 and 11 live, and the comparison sheet beside it.

`index.html` — the panel sets the attributes on `<html>`; `round10.css`
and `round11.css` do the rest. The presets R1–R4 are the direction pages;
the state is in the address; `panel=off` hides the panel for embedding.
The card placement is greyed unless the logo is on the card; the logo
height and the mix are greyed when there is no logo.

`compare.html` — Q6 (round 10's recommendation, with the poster) as the
reference, dashed, then the four at half the 1920 render.

The wordmarks are stand-ins built by `make-round-11` from each band's own
title text: one treatment per title in the fonts this machine has
(Nimbus, URW, Roboto Condensed, C059, P052, Z003), some two-line, some
tinted, sized to the logo box by height and by character count so a long
name never overflows its box. They stand for the titles' logo PNGs; the
layout and the mix are the subject.
