# N · Tier approaches

J's three rail cards (You, Cleo, Nick; posters 96×144; the ink disc 44 with the solid glyph 28; the 44×78 capsule for two acts) drawn once per approach. Same acts, same counts every row: four at 1, three at 2, three at 3+; Cleo's Charade is heart 3+ over eye 1. The heart is white throughout — colour is the grade's. Read at half size and in 2× crops of Cleo's card.

## Per row

**1 · Fill.** 1 and 2 are the same disc by design; 3+ is a glance at half size, gold on ink. Costs nothing beyond J's disc. Reads as a mark: the disc it already was, coloured.

**2 · Numeral.** The 20px numeral survives half size (10px, one digit, 600 weight). But a counted single act takes the two-act capsule's shape, 44×78 — 54% of the poster's height; heart · 3 · eye runs 112, 78%. The numeral sits between two glyphs, so which it counts is a read, not a glance. It is the notification badge, and reads as one.

**3 · Stack.** 1 vs stacked is a look on a light poster; on a dark one (Cosmos, Charade) the ink crescent behind an ink disc is 3px at half size and disappears. 2 vs 3+ needs the crescents counted — not at half. The 12px offset carries the back disc past the poster's edge (the disc is inset 8). A capsule stacks whole at its higher tier; the approach cannot grade two acts apart. Reads as a pile of chips, not a mark.

**4 · Size.** 44/56/68 tell apart when neighbours give a reference; alone, 56 reads as 44. 68 is 71% of the poster's width; on the capsule a 68 heart over a 44 eye is a snowman. Reads as a badge, the largest one.

**5 · Heavy ring.** Three tiers at half size: plain, a 2px gold rim, gold fill — each a glance. The ring reads as colour where M's 2px white ring read as an edge. Costs nothing beyond J's disc. Reads as a mark, though a rimmed disc leans toward a medal.

## Pick

Heavy ring: three legible tiers at J's footprint, the amount of gold as the count — none, rim, full. Runner-up: Fill — two tiers, the same footprint, and gold stays rare; if 2 friends does not need its own step, Fill is the quieter answer. On a roster where 2 is common the ring rims most discs and gold stops meaning much; Fill's cost is its two steps.

Rule out: Size. It cannot be read without a neighbour, it takes the poster, and it breaks the capsule.
