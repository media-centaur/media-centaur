# T4 · Open rows, 1500 — reasoning

**Knobs** surface `open` (the app's ground, no unit ground) · width `narrow` · poster 100 · logo none.

## Style

The rows on the page with nothing around them: hairlines between, no
container, no ink. The app's ground shows through everywhere; the posters
and tiles are the only shapes. Narrowed to 1500.

## Decisions

* **No surface at all.** Round 10's Q3 without the still: if a container is
  one box too many, this is the list with none.
* **Hairlines only.** They are the whole structure; the rail's cards are
  rows the same way.

## Requirements

* *No backdrop* — none.
* *Like the app* — the app's ground and type; the container is the one
  thing it drops.
* *Narrower columns* — 1500.

## Trade-offs

Gains: the lightest page; nothing encloses anything. Costs: without the
container the two columns have no edge and read as one field of rows and
cards; the rail and the feed are told apart only by their content. In the
app: round 10's open rules with the still removed.
