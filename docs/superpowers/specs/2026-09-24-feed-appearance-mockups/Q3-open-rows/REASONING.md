# Q3 · Open rows — reasoning

**Knobs** ground `app` · unit `open` · edges `sheet` · hairline `on` · still `box` · dissolve 360.

## Style

Nothing has a ground. The page is today's — base-100, the blobs, the app's
scrim — and a band is a row on it: tile, poster, words, the time, with the
still a soft-edged photograph at the row's right fading into the page over
360px. Hairlines between rows. The rail is a list of people, not cards. No
text shadow: the words sit on plain ground as they do everywhere else.

## Decisions

* **Remove the figure, not darken the ground.** The other side of the
  inversion: if the unit has no tone of its own it cannot be darker than
  the page. This is Watch History's list surface stretched to the band's
  height.
* **The still keeps the box and takes a longer dissolve.** Without ink to
  hand the picture to, its left edge fades straight into a lighter ground;
  360 instead of 240 softens the seam. The still cannot run under the words
  here — there is nothing to put them on.
* **Hover is a fill, not a lift.** White at 5% on the row; the cursor ring
  as everywhere.

## Requirements

* *Less boxes* — zero boxes; nothing is enclosed.
* *A good idea* — the page keeps the app's tone and idiom exactly; the
  least foreign of the six.
* *Couch* — the words read as on any app page; the stills are the settled
  size.

## Trade-offs

Gains: no new ground, no new surface, the lightest change in kind. Costs:
the least cinematic; a dark still's blacks against the blue-grey ground make
a soft dark region at the row's right (Metropolis, the Charade car
interior), a softened version of the hole the round set out to remove; the
band's ink scrim, which the mockup rounds tuned, is gone. In the app: the
band and card grounds to transparent, the scrim off, the sheet rules, the
mask at 360.
