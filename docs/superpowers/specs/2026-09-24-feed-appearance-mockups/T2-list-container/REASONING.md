# T2 · List surface, the 1280 container — reasoning

**Knobs** surface `list` · width `container` (1280: feed 776, rail 480) · poster 80 · logo none.

## Style

T1 pulled in to the container every other page uses, left-aligned as they
are: the feed at 776, the rail at 480 with the same person card. The poster
steps down to 80×120 so the words keep their measure. The row stays 206.

## Decisions

* **The app's container.** Discovery opted out of the 1280 container for
  the cinematic feed; with the still gone the reason is gone, and the page
  can rejoin the others.
* **The smaller poster.** At 776 a 100px poster takes a seventh of the row;
  80 gives the words 20px back and reads as the list's thumbnail rather
  than its subject.
* **The rail at 480.** The person card's three posters and their gaps fit;
  nothing in the card changes.

## Requirements

* *No backdrop* — none.
* *Like the app* — the same container, alignment and surface as Library,
  History and Settings.
* *Narrower columns* — the narrowest of the round.

## Trade-offs

Gains: the page most like the app around it; the densest column (five rows
above the fold); the rail's card unchanged. Costs: reviews wrap to three
lines more often at 776 and the third is clipped; 540px of a 1920 panel is
empty at the right, as on every other page; the smaller poster reads less
from the couch. In the app: as T1 with the container at its default and
the poster derivative at 160.
