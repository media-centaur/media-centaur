# Crop measurement — real backdrops at the band's slice and the lead's box

2026-09-24, the critique's gate before round 5. Sample: the 24 backdrops
in the install's referenced artwork tier (`~/.local/share/media-centaur/images/tmdb/*/backdrop.jpg`,
every one a real TMDB backdrop for a title a friend or the owner has
acted on; the library's own entity backdrops are stored under a
different path and were not needed once 24 real stills gave a clear
answer). Pages and derivatives were generated by a scratch script
(`make-measure`, not committed) with `vips thumbnail` and
`vips smartcrop`; the pages are published in the review artifact under
*Measurement*, not in the repo, because they carry the owner's own
artwork.

Geometry, from direction 1 and direction 4:

| Box | At 1920 | Image scaled to | Visible slice of the still |
|---|---|---|---|
| Band, full width | 1260×152 (from x=560) | 1260×709 | **21%** |
| Band, image box capped | 900×152 | 900×506 | **30%** |
| Band, 1280 container | 720×152 | 720×405 | 37% |
| Lead | 1280×300 | 1280×720 | **42%** |

Three treatments: a fixed `object-position: 50% p%` (CSS, no server
work); libvips `smartcrop --interesting attention`; `--interesting
entropy`. Judged by eye at half scale: **S** the slice shows the still's
subject (a face, a figure, the object the frame is about); **A** the
still has no subject at that slice and the band reads as atmosphere
(a dark cave, a landscape, a planet); **F** the slice cuts the subject
so it reads wrong (a face cut at the eyes, backs of heads, a ceiling).

## Band, 21% slice, 24 stills

| Treatment | S | A | F | The failures |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| fixed 35% | 16 | 5 | 3 | faces cut at the eyes on three stills where the faces sit high (rows 4, 6, 10) |
| attention | 15 | 5 | 4 | a beard and collar under a face (7), rocks beside a figure (14), a ceiling above a man (18), the backs of a crowd's heads (22) |
| entropy | 15 | 5 | 4 | 7, 22, 24 (window tops) and 11 (hands, not faces) |

Fixed and attention fail on **different** stills: fixed fails when the
subject sits high; attention fails on busy or low-contrast frames.
Neither rescues the other, and attention costs a server-side crop per
derivative. Attention is rejected.

## Fixed-position sweep, band, 24 stills

| Position | Failures at the 21% slice | Failures at the 30% slice (900px box) |
|---|---|---|
| 25% | 2 (hats and foreheads on the crowd shots, 22 and 23) | — |
| **30%** | **0** (row 6 shows torsos under the faces — marginal, not wrong) | **0** |
| 35% | 2–3 (4, 6; 10 marginal) | 0 |
| 40% | 4+ (chins and mouths: 7, 15, 21, 24) | — |

TMDB backdrops are composed for 16:9 with the subject's eyes in the
upper third; a slice anchored at 30% from the top catches them. The
five "atmosphere" stills are atmosphere at every position and every
treatment — the still has no subject at 152px and the poster beside it
carries identity, which is the design's intent.

## Lead, 42% slice, 24 stills

Fixed 35%: 24 S or A, 0 F. Attention: 4 F (the same 7, 14, 18, 22).
The lead is forgiving; any fixed position between 30% and 35% works.

## Decision carried into round 5

- **One rule, no hand-tuning: `object-position: 50% 30%`** on every
  backdrop, band and lead. Round-5 mockups must use it as is; a
  designer may not set a per-title position, because the app cannot.
- **No smart crop.** Not better, not simpler.
- **The slice height is a real lever.** Full width (21%) is at the
  edge of what a fixed position can serve; a capped image box (30%)
  or the 1280 container (37%) is safer. Round 5 renders both and the
  owner sees the difference on the same rows.
- **Adjacent stills of one title**: offset the second by 8% (38%) so
  two rows on one title do not repeat one frame; still within the
  safe range measured here.
