## The strip's rule

One poster per **title acted on**, newest first, left to right; every act on it is a flag on its poster, in mast order (Cleo's Charade: heart over eye). The ago is the first poster's. A title without artwork is the 6% slot, named, flying its flag (Femi's Coffee Run). A binge collapses: three episodes of one show are one poster with one eye; the episode is the opened card's.

## The holes

From the render: every card with any act is **335 tall** — one row of posters, two or five — so the grid has **one hole, beside Theo**: 211px under Femi. At thirty friends: a hole only beside a friend who has shared nothing, always 211. The ink right of a short strip (422px on Sam's) is inside the card, which the head spans. The 1080 fold holds two rows and the third's heads; 1280 one card and the second's head; 2560 (capped at 1820) three rows.

## The 10-foot check

At half, per flag: the **heart** reads as rose before it reads as a heart; the **eye** is a ring with a dot, nothing else is; the **bubble** keeps its tail and two lines; the **thumb** keeps its stem and reads up from down by where it sits; the **bookmark** keeps its notch; two flags read as two. Every name, ago and initial, the ring (2px at half, with the lift), the opened rows and the rail's 28 glyph read. **It changed nothing**: the sizes are H's and I's, each already tuned at half. The owner's decision did change the page: with nothing under the name, the name centres on the tile.
