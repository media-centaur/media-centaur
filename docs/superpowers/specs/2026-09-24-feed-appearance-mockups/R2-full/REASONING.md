Round 7: `F-cinematic-feed` re-derived at the couch floors, full width, no rail — the fair comparison for R1 and R3. Nothing re-opened but size: C's band, A2's lead, the crop rule and the scrim recipe stand. No JavaScript; generated by a scratch script (not committed) so `.alt` is derived from adjacency. Rendered three times at 1920, once at 1280 and 2560, plus the 10-foot check's half-size copies.

## What the user sees

One unit at two sizes, 6px apart on the page ground, full width (1820 at 1920): tile, poster, text block (sentence · title and year · review), the seat at the poster's foot, the time, the picture.

**The text zone's edge** is `clamp(640px, width − 940px, 880px)`: **880 at 1920** (the width less the band's 900px box and a 40px gutter — C's 344px gutter closes), retreating to 640 so a 540px box survives at 1280. The dissolve stays 360; the box's left edge is `max(tz, width − cap)` as in F. The lead's words run 150px onto the dissolve (F: 224).

## The 10-foot check — what it changed

Half-size copies of the fold, the hovered seats, the cursor column and the Watchlist:

1. Every read text, title, time and tile letter read at half size; the faintest, the year at F's 60%, was **lifted to 66%**.
2. **The cursor was not obvious.** F's 3px ring read as a hairline and the 16% lift was barely there. The ring went to **4px**, the lift to **17% with the scrim × .72** — one rule for hover and cursor, since the cursor is the TV's hover.
3. Plain states at 66% and the Watchlist's flags read; unchanged.
4. Two full-size fixes: the 20px sentiment glyph read oversized beside 22px text — 18 on the band, 22 on the lead; F's `drop-shadow` filter on the lead title painted a dark halo on the ink at 44px — removed.
5. The `.state` labels fail at half size; they are scaffolding, not the app.

## The Watchlist at full width (state 12)

## 1280 and 2560

**1280×800.** Units 1180; the text zone retreats to 640; the band's box is 540 (the dissolve plus 180px clear), a whole-height slice. The measure is 398px (33ch): reviews wrap to two lines and a long title ellipsizes ("Night of the Living Dead" at 28px is ≈403px). The lead's box 540×404 shows the still's whole height — F's "card beside a headline". The tab strip is unchanged; there is no rail to fold. The desk's remedy is the UI scale preference.

**2560×1440.** Units 2460; the text zone clamps at 880; 680px of ink before the band's picture — C's known limit, moot where the app renders 2560 near 1920 CSS px. The lead's box is 1580×404, 45%.

## What a new arrival does

The new action becomes the lead; the old lead becomes band 1 — its still re-crops from 76% in a 940px box to 47% in a 900px box whose left edge moves 880 → 920, its poster to 120×180, its title to 28px — and everything below moves down 242px. The lead's height is fixed, so the top holds. `.alt` re-evaluates on render; an empty You has no lead.

## The one thing I am least sure of

The lead's box. At 940×404 it is a 1.44:1 window on a 16:9 still — a 76% slice — so the lead reads as a large card beside a headline and the 47% bands are the more cinematic crop; in F the lead (1244×340) was the sliver. The text zone at 880 took 300px of the lead's picture. The alternative is a lead-only box start at 640 (1180px, a 61% slice) with the words 390px onto the dissolve, at the cost of a second image-box rule. I kept one rule; the owner should judge the lead against F's on the same still.
