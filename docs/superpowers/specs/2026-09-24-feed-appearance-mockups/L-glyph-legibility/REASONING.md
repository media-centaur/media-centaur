# L · Glyph legibility

J's rail cards (You, Cleo, Nick; 560 wide, posters 96×144) drawn three times, once per treatment of the act glyph, then Cleo's page card (900; posters 130×195) once in (b). Only the flag's CSS and the sprite change between rows. Judged from `half.png` (the 1920 render at .5) and `cleo-{a,b,c,d}-2x.png`.

Heart `--love` throughout. Band ink .9 per the brief; K drew .85.

**Capsule.** In (a) and (b) the backing is the `.flags` box: `--d` wide, padded `(--d − --g)/2`, glyphs 6 apart, ends fully round. One act is the 44 disc (52 on the page); two acts one capsule of **44×78** on the rail, **52×90** on the page. Drawn on Cleo's Charade (heart over eye) and, in the page row, Caligari (bubble over eye).

## At half size

**(a) outline.** Heart and eye read. Thumbs up and down are 1px hairlines at 14px: the hand is recognisable, its direction only on inspection — Nick's thumbs-up and You's thumbs-down look alike at a glance. The bookmark is a faint open outline on Big Buck Bunny's light art. The .85 ink lets Pioneer One's helmet bleed through under the eye.

**(b) solid.** Every glyph reads at a glance: the eye's pupil hole, the thumb's mass low (up) or high (down), the bookmark's solid tag with its notch. Bubble vs eye — page row only, since no rail card here carries a review — is unambiguous: a blob with a tail against an almond with a hole.

**(c) band.** Legibility equals (b), marginally better from the drop shadow. Over Big Buck Bunny's white art the scrim goes mid-grey and muddies.

## What the art keeps

(a) and (b) cost the same footprint — a 44 disc is ~11% of the poster, the 44×78 capsule ~20%, top-right, where posters are usually quiet (Charade's figure is the exception). The .85 ink was already near-opaque over art; 1.0 loses nothing visible. (c) takes the bottom 25% of every poster, where titles live: CHARADE loses its lower half, the thumb covers "cosmos laundromat", Pioneer One's laurels vanish.

## Pick

**(b).** It fixes (a)'s one fault — hairline glyphs at couch distance — at no extra cost to the art, and beats (c) on art survival by a wide margin for a negligible legibility difference. The two-act capsule reads as one mark.

Caveat: the bubble is absent from the rail rows because the three cards are verbatim; judging it at 96×144 needs a rail card with a review.
