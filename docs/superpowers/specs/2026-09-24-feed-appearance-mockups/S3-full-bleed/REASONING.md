# S3 · Full bleed, time at the edge — reasoning

**Knobs** picture `full` · time `edge` · logo `headline` (72) · on Q6's page.

## Style

The still under the whole band, the words on the left under the round-10
"full" scrim, and the time at the top right over a vignette that darkens
the band's last 300px, borrowed from the round-5 lead, which put its time
in the same corner over the same device. The picture is not on the right
because it is everywhere.

## Decisions

* **Keep the still large.** The other three compositions shrink the
  picture; this one keeps the cinematic band and answers the two
  complaints without giving that up.
* **The vignette carries the time.** An 18px time over a bright frame
  fails; over a .5 ink vignette with the text shadow it reads, as the lead
  showed.
* **The words keep their zone.** They stay on the heavy left of the scrim;
  only the time leaves it.

## Requirements

* *Not the image on the right* — read as "not a box on the right": the
  picture is the band.
* *Not the time in the middle* — the time is at the corner.
* *A good idea* — it is Q2's and R4's presence with the one repair the
  owner asked for.

## Trade-offs

Gains: the largest picture; the page stays a reel. Costs: the crop rule
stays (a 38% slice at 264, faces sometimes cut); the right-edge vignette
darkens the picture's clearest part; if the owner's dislike is of a large
picture at the right rather than of a box there, this does not answer it.
In the app: the round-10 "full" scrim plus a right-edge gradient, the
time's anchor at the band's right.
