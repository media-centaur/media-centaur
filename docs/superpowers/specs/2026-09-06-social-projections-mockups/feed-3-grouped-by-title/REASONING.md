# Feed 3 · One row per title, named pennants

**Style.** Today's row, but grouped: one row per title, positioned by its newest recommendation. The lead names everyone ("Bob, Cleo · 2d ago"), the mast flies a named pennant per friend, and when more than one friend left a note the secondary line becomes a short list of attributed notes instead of one clamped note.

**Decisions.** A title is the unit you act on, so it should appear once. Grouping also makes the pennants say who, which the modal already does; the Feed's icon-only pennants existed because the lead named the one sender. Four rows here for the same five recommendations as the other mockups.

**Trade-offs.** Gains: no duplicate rows, the "two friends agree" signal is visible at a glance, the row stays shared with the watchlist. Costs: a title with three notes grows tall, and re-sorting a row to the top when a second friend adds to it moves an item the reader may already have dismissed in their head.
