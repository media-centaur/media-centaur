3 — Scrim and seek bar: the familiar TV player

The bar is what every TV player shows: a bottom gradient scrim, the title above a full-width seek bar with a handle and chapter ticks, the clock at both ends. Pausing also drops a large glyph in the centre for a moment. Prompts sit at the top right so they never meet the bar. The track menu is a centred modal with two columns, the same shape as the desktop's TAB menu, over a dimmed picture.
