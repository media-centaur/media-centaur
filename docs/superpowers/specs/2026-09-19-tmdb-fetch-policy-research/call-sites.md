## `ReleaseTracking.Refresher` — full schedule

- **Two independent timers**, both armed in `init/1` (`refresher.ex:65-87`) with a **10-second first-tick floor** (`@first_tick_floor_ms`, `:36`) and drift correction through `RefreshSchedule.next_delay_ms/4` (`refresh_schedule.ex:31-36`).
- **Refresh cycle** — `handle_info(:refresh, …)` `refresher.ex:90`. Interval: `Settings.Config.get(:release_tracking_refresh_interval_hours) || 6` hours (`:346-349`). Last-completed anchor is `max(last_refreshed_at)` over `ReleaseTracking.Item` (`:390-395`).
  - Items re-read: **every** row from `ReleaseTracking.list_all_items()` (`:162`) — no filtering by tracked-ness (the row's existence *is* the follow), no future-air-date filter, no staleness filter.
  - Phase 1: `Task.Supervisor.async_stream_nolink`, `max_concurrency: 4`, `timeout: 30_000`, `on_timeout: :kill_task` (`:166-173`).
  - Endpoints per item: `tv/{id}` **+ `tv/{id}/season/{n}`** for the seasons `Helpers.seasons_to_fetch/2` picks — `max(last_library_season, 1)` plus `next_episode_to_air`'s season when it is higher (`helpers.ex:90-98`); or `collection/{id}`; or `movie/{id}` (`refresher.ex:256-293`).
  - `reload: true` on **the three detail calls only** (`:257, :279, :289`) — rationale at `:247-249` ("TMDB marks details fresh for about eight hours, longer than the refresh interval"). The **season fetches carry no reload** (`helpers.ex:131`), so a season detail can be answered from the HTTP response cache.
  - Skip conditions: per item, `if IntegrationAvailability.up?(:tmdb), do: …, else: {:held, item}` (`:252-254`) — the *only* per-item skip. Everything else is fetched every cycle.
  - Phase 2: serialized commits, `replace_releases!` + `mark_in_library_releases` + `sync_wants` (`:297-308`), then `update_item_metadata` writing `last_refreshed_at` and the self-healing identity columns (`:310-329`).
  - Phase 3: bounded artwork backfill, `max_concurrency: 4`, `timeout: 60_000` (`:235-245`).
  - Publishes `{:releases_updated, changed_ids}` (`:207-212`).
  - **Hold behaviour:** a tick that finds `:tmdb` unavailable spends nothing and re-arms at `ProbeJob.cadence_seconds() * 1_000` = **300 s** (`:93`, `:336`), not the full 6 h. A `{:integration_availability_changed, :tmdb, :up}` broadcast runs the deferred cycle immediately (`:104-108`).
  - A raise/exit inside a tick is contained by `tick/2` (`:139-157`); the cadence survives.
- **Want-ledger sweep** — `handle_info(:sweep, …)` `refresher.ex:116`. Interval: `Settings.Config.get(:release_tracking_sweep_interval_minutes) || 15` minutes (`:351-354`). **Touches TMDB not at all** — it calls `ReleaseTracking.sync_wants/1` per item and publishes `{:tracking_sweep_completed}` (`:356-375`). Anchor persisted in Settings key `"release_tracking:last_swept_at"` (`:35`, `:397-415`).

## `MediaCentaur.TmdbArtwork`

- **Stores:** three files per referenced identity under `{data_dir}/images/tmdb/{movie|tv_series}-{tmdb_id}/` — `poster.jpg`, `backdrop.jpg`, `logo.png` (`tmdb_artwork.ex:60`, `:367-369`, `:100-108`). Masters are TMDB `original`; serving sizes come from the `?w=` derivative ladder (`:64-66`). DB stores nothing — **disk is the ledger**; `urls/2` is a `File.exists?` check per role (`:150-154`).
- **TTL / retention sweep:** `@ttl_days 7` (`:61`). `sweep/0` (`:261-276`) deletes an entry only when **both** hold: no registered hold on its `{media_type, tmdb_id}`, and directory mtime older than `now - 7 days`. Derivatives are purged with the master (`:315-322`). Registered as retention policy `:tmdb_artwork`, `mode: :sweep`, `run: &TmdbArtwork.sweep/0` (`tmdb_artwork/retention_policies.ex:14-24`), run by `Retention.SweepJob` on Oban cron **`"33 4 * * *"` (daily, 04:33)** (`config/config.exs:103`).
- **Holds** (`tmdb_artwork/hold_provider.ex:18`), registered at `config/config.exs:164-169`:
  - `ReleaseTracking.TmdbArtworkHolds` — every `release_tracking_items` row (`release_tracking/tmdb_artwork_holds.ex:13-17`).
  - `Acquisition.TmdbArtworkHolds` — every pursuit in `State.in_flight()` with both `tmdb_id` and `tmdb_type` (`acquisition/tmdb_artwork_holds.ex:16-33`).
  - `Discovery.TmdbArtworkHolds` — every `TitleIntent` with `rung != :ignored` (`discovery/tmdb_artwork_holds.ex:13-17`).
  - `Activities.TmdbArtworkHolds` — every `activities` row (`activities/tmdb_artwork_holds.ex:13-17`).
- **Last-used stamp:** directory mtime, bumped by `touch/2` from `ensure/2` (`:179`) and from every successful `download_role` (`:358`).
- **Re-download triggers:** (a) `ensure/2` when any of the three roles is missing on disk (`:169-187`) — gated on `IntegrationAvailability.up?(:tmdb)` at `:177`; (b) `Helpers.download_images_sync/3` from onboarding and every refresh cycle, for roles not on disk (`helpers.ex:47-59`); (c) `refresh_if_stale/4` when the on-disk file is under **50 000 bytes** (`@stale_threshold_bytes`, `:71`, `:222-248`) — used only by the mix task. Nothing deletes an entry synchronously; untracking leaves it to age out (`:29-33`).

## `MediaCentaur.TMDB.ProbeJob`

- Oban worker, queue `:maintenance` (concurrency 1), `max_attempts: 3`, `unique: [period: :infinity, states: [:available, :scheduled, :executing, :retryable]]` (`probe_job.ex:25-28`).
- **Interval: 300 seconds** (`@cadence_seconds 300`, `:36`) — snoozes at that cadence while `:tmdb` is still down, completes on up (`:55`).
- **Endpoint:** `GET /3/configuration` via `Client.configuration()` (`:53`), which forces `reload: true` (`client.ex:99`) so it is never answered from the response cache.
- Not a cron job: enqueued reactively by `Availability.enqueue_probe/0` on a down transition, `schedule_in: 300` (`availability.ex:54-64`). **Nothing probes while TMDB is up** (`probe_job.ex:16-18`).
- Completes without probing when `Capabilities.tmdb_ready?()` is false (`:46`). The same `cadence_seconds/0` is the Refresher's hold interval (`refresher.ex:336`).

## "Last fetched" timestamps on TMDB-derived records

There is exactly **one**: `ReleaseTracking.Item.last_refreshed_at` (`release_tracking/item.ex:42`, castable at `:84` and `:107`).

- Written by `Onboarding.onboard/2` at creation (`onboarding.ex:66`, `:93`) and by `Refresher.update_item_metadata/3` on every cycle (`refresher.ex:317`).
- Read by exactly one caller: `Refresher.last_refresh_completed_at/0` — `Repo.aggregate(ReleaseTracking.Item, :max, :last_refreshed_at)` (`refresher.ex:390-395`), used only to compute the first timer delay after boot. It is never read per-item, never used to decide whether to skip an item, and never surfaced in the UI.

Adjacent, non-TMDB timestamps for contrast: `"release_tracking:last_swept_at"` in the Settings table (`refresher.ex:35`, read at `:377-388`), and `IntegrationHealth.Status.last_tested_at` / `Capabilities.save_test_result` for the `configuration` probe result (`integration_health.ex:249`, `:264`). No library entity (`Movie`, `TVSeries`, `Season`, `Episode`, `MovieSeries`) carries any fetched-at/synced-at column — freshness there is inferred from content instead (empty cast/crew at `maintenance.ex:208`, incomplete `episode_list` at `maintenance.ex:330`), and `TmdbArtwork` uses directory mtime rather than a row.
