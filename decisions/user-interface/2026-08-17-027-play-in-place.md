# Play affordances play in place — the modal is never a waystation

## Context and Problem Statement

Starting playback with a mouse took two aimed clicks: open the detail modal, find Play. The owner's weighting is the opposite: a click on a title should mostly play it. The only one-click path, `autoplay=1`, opened the modal and then fired play, so the modal was a waystation.

## Decision Outcome

1. **Playing happens in place**, from the surface the user is on, with no detail modal before, during, or after. The hero's Play fires playback directly; "More info" remains the modal's front door.
2. **Every playable card surface** (library grid, Recently Added, Continue Watching) reveals a centered circular play button (`Components.PlayOverlay`) after a hover-intent delay (`--play-overlay-reveal-delay`); the fade-out is never delayed. The button plays; any other click on the card opens details. A soft radial halo behind the button, never a card-wide scrim.
3. **Pointer-gated only.** Never keyboard or gamepad focus, including programmatic `:focus-visible`. Not a nav item; no layout or scroll change.
4. **Playability decides visibility**, per the library views' presence vocabulary. Collection shelf cards and "See all" cards get none (UIDR-025).
5. **One action on the hover layer.** No add-to-list, ratings, or metadata.
6. **Default-on off-switch**: Settings → Preferences → "Play button on cards" (`card_play_button`). Off restores the modal as the only route from a card to Play.
7. **Modal-then-play is retired**: the `autoplay` URL param, the modal's deferred-autoplay step, and the continue-watching `autoplay` flag are gone.

### Considered and rejected

* Whole card plays, or split zones: inverts "card click opens details", makes every stray click launch fullscreen mpv (plus an HDR mode flip on the TV path), and splits mouse-click and gamepad-SELECT meanings.
* Dwell-then-expand pop-out: duplicates the modal at the highest motion cost; only its hover-intent delay was adopted.

### Consequences

* Play from a card skips the modal's pre-play context; the resolver's choice is trusted. Touch input is out of scope.
