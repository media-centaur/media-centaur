The watchlist is the list of titles you want to watch, at **Discovery → Watchlist**. You put
things on it and you take them off; nothing else does. It is also where you arm release
tracking, so a title you want and a title the app is watching for are one record.

Adding a title costs nothing — no search, no download, no calendar. Arming is a second,
deliberate act.

## Adding a title

- **From a search** — every result row in [search & download](/guide/search-and-download)
  carries a bookmark.
- **From a friend's recommendation** — **Add to watchlist** on the Discovery feed keeps who it
  came from.
- **From any title's detail** — the same bookmark sits with the other controls.

A saved title keeps the name, year, poster and synopsis it had when you saved it, so the list
renders when TMDB is unreachable. Titles you already own can stay on the list — owning
something isn't the same as having watched it — and you can take any row off at any time.

## Tracking modes

Open a row and the **Tracking** control sets what the app does about it. Choosing anything
above **Off** starts tracking the title, and puts it on your watchlist if it isn't there yet.

| Mode | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Off** | Nothing. The title stays on your list; no calendar is kept and nothing is grabbed |
| **Watch** | New releases appear under **Coming up**; nothing is downloaded |
| **Ask** | A release drops and a draft plan parks on Incoming for you to approve |
| **Grab** | A release drops and it's downloaded |
| **Default** | Follows your global auto-grab setting, and changes when you change it |

A watchlist entry you arm starts at **Watch**, never at your global default — putting
something on a list should not start a download. A series the app picks up on its own, because
you own it, starts at **Default**.

**Off is remembered.** Turn tracking off for a title and it stays off, even if you delete the
title from your library and acquire it again later. Nothing but you turns it back on.

## What gets tracked

- **A series you own.** New seasons are picked up without you asking, at your global setting.
- **Anything you arm.** A film that isn't out yet, a show you don't have — set a mode and it's
  followed.
- **Leftovers from a download.** Episodes a plan couldn't get are handed to tracking, and the
  title joins your watchlist.

Delete a series from your library and tracking stops — unless you armed it yourself, in which
case it stays on your watchlist and keeps going at the mode you set. If you don't want that,
take it off the list.
