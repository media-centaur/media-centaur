# A review replaces the recommendation

## Glossary

Existing terms are in [`docs/GLOSSARY.md`](../docs/GLOSSARY.md): *activity*,
*action*, *listing*, *feed*, *feed entry*, *pennant*, *tombstone*, *sharing
toggle*, *title intent*, *rung*, *Ignored*. This campaign retires
*recommendation* and adds:

* **Review** — one person's opinion of one title, told to their friends: an
  optional sentiment and optional text, either, both or neither. Activity
  kind 32164, `Activity.kind` `:review`. One per person per title; a newer
  one replaces the earlier one everywhere, and a deletion withdraws it.
  Replaces the recommendation (kind 32160, retired).
* **Sentiment** — the verdict a review carries: *dislike* (thumbs down),
  *like* (thumbs up), *love* (heart), or none. Wire field `sentiment`,
  values `dislike` / `like` / `love`; absent means none. Formerly two-valued
  with absent meaning like.
* **Review text** — the review's words, up to 500 characters. Wire and row
  field `text`. Formerly *note*.
* **Review modal** — the form: the title, the three sentiment choices (none
  preselected; the chosen one can be cleared), the text, the relay line,
  Send and Cancel. `Live.ReviewModal` rendering `Live.ReviewFlow`'s state.
  Formerly the Recommend modal.
* **Review control** — the control on a title's surfaces that opens the
  modal: the library detail's view controls and the title detail modal's
  action strip. Gated by `show_discovery`, as Recommend was.
* **Reviewed flag** — the pennant a review with no sentiment flies:
  "Nick reviewed this". A review with a sentiment flies that sentiment.

## Goal

"Recommend" becomes "review". A recommendation was advice with a positive
floor — its sentiment was Like or Love and an absent one meant Like. A
review is an opinion of any valence: thumbs down, thumbs up, heart, or no
verdict, with or without words. The new activity kind replaces the old one
on the wire, in storage, in every control and every sentence, so that
nothing in the app says "recommend" once it ships.

### Facts that decide the design

* **The relay never reads content.** `kinds.go` stores by kind number;
  the content schema is the app's alone. Changing what a kind's content
  means costs nothing at the relay; changing the kind number costs a relay
  release (v0.6.0) — and the protocol says which of the two this is.
* **The protocol's own rules.** A new kind takes the next free number in
  the block: 32164. Numbers are never reused. A retired kind is refused by
  relays and dropped by readers. A change that alters the meaning of an
  existing field bumps `v`. Absent `sentiment` today means *like*;
  tomorrow it means *none*. That is a change of meaning, and the message's
  whole claim changes with it (advice → opinion), so it is a new kind, not
  a field edit under a version bump.
* **The precedent is one day old.** ADR-067 retired 32162: new kind,
  relay release first, stored rows of the retired kind deleted by a data
  migration (`20260911120000_listing_replaces_tracking.exs`), old-version
  installs out of step until they update. This campaign follows it.
* **What the real database holds** (read through `Activities.list_activities/0`,
  2026-09-12): 20 live activities — 11 recommendations (9 own, 6 of them
  with a note; 2 from friends; sentiments 9 like, 2 love), 7 watched,
  2 listings. 2 friends, 1 relay.
* **SQLite has no `ALTER COLUMN`** (`ecto_sqlite3` raises on `modify`).
  `sentiment` (not null, default like) becomes nullable by drop-and-add,
  which is safe because every row that carries a sentiment is a
  recommendation and the data migration removes those. The `kind`
  column's default `"recommendation"` cannot be dropped without rebuilding
  the table; the changeset always sets `kind`, so the default is never
  read.
* **One sentiment, three glyph maps.** `Pennant.glyph/1`,
  `PersonCard.sentiment_glyph/1` and the inline heart in `FeedEntryCard`
  each spell the sentiment → icon rule. A third value would make that
  three sites × three values.
* **The Feed encodes Like as nothing.** UIDR-038 §1: "a rose heart for
  Love and nothing for Like". With a real *none*, absence must mean none.
* **The dev friend cannot send a sentiment.** `mix social.dev recommend`
  has `--note` but no sentiment flag, so every scripted recommendation is
  a like. Reviews need all three exercised end to end.
* **Two docs already drifted.** The Status widget's attrs say
  "recommendations sent / received" but `Activities.counts/0` counts every
  kind; the JS input comments still name the Recommendations tab.

## Design (unify pass)

### Core idea

**A review is one person's opinion of one title, told to their friends — a
verdict, words, both, or neither — and it is the one thing a person says
about a title on purpose.** The three activity kinds then read as opinion,
fact and intent: a review (what I think, said deliberately), a title
watched (what I did, shared by toggle), a listing (what I want, shared by
toggle). The recommendation was opinion plus advice with a positive floor;
the review drops the advice and the floor. The reader decides what to do
with it.

### Greenfield shape

**Wire — kind 32164, Review.** Addressable; the same `d` address and the
same envelope (`v`, `title`) as every activity.

| Field | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `sentiment` | `"dislike"` / `"like"` / `"love"` | Absent means none. Any other value is malformed. |
| `text` | string or null, 500 characters | |
| `reviewed_at` | Unix seconds | When the person reviewed it. Absent means `created_at`. |

A deletion's `a` tag may name 32164, 32161 or 32163. Both subscriptions
ask for 32164, 32161, 32163 and 5. 32160 is retired: never reused,
refused by relays with `blocked:`, dropped by readers as `:wrong_kind`.
The `p` tag stays reserved, now "for a directed review". Relay v0.6.0.

**Row.** `kind` `:review | :watched | :listing`. `sentiment` nullable
`:dislike | :like | :love` — nil on a review with no verdict and on every
other kind's row, which removes today's "default, never read" wart. `text`
in place of `note`. `acted_at` holds `reviewed_at`. One schema migration
(rename `note` → `text`; drop and re-add `sentiment` nullable without a
default) and one data migration deleting `kind = 'recommendation'` rows,
append-only, idempotent, with a test in the style of
`listing_replaces_tracking_test.exs`.

**Context.** `Activities.review(title, sentiment | nil, text | nil)` —
always publishes, mints the identity, trims the text, rejects over 500
with `{:error, :text_too_long}`. `recommend/3` is deleted.
`Activity.sentiments/0` is `[:dislike, :like, :love]`. `Translation`
emits `sentiment` only when there is one and reads an absent one as nil.
`friend_activity_for/1` keeps own reviews in (as own recommendations were):
"You dislike this" on a title is worth seeing. `Sync` says "rejected a
review".

**Web.** `Live.ReviewFlow` and `Live.ReviewModal` (assigns `review_subject`,
`review_poster_url`, `review_relay_counts`; events `review_cancel`,
`review_send`; hosts `modal_review_open` and `title_review_open`; attr
`review?`). The modal's sentiment choice is three toggles, none pressed at
open, a second press clearing the one pressed — a radio group cannot be
cleared, so this is buttons over one hidden input, each a nav item. Send
is always enabled. One `Components.Title.Sentiment` renders a sentiment
glyph everywhere a sentiment shows — the modal's choices, the pennant, the
feed entry, the Friends card — with its story. The pennant flies six
flags in mast order love · like · dislike · reviewed · watched · listing,
love the one colour; `flag/1` maps a review to its sentiment or, without
one, to `:review`. A feed entry reads "Nick reviewed" then the glyph when
there is a sentiment, then the text when there is text; a bare review is
the name, the verb and the title. The Friends card's shelf is *Reviewed*.
The review control is `hero-pencil-square` named *Review* on both
surfaces (the paper plane said "send"; the verb is now review).

**Words.** Verb "reviewed", noun "review", pennant tooltips "Nick loves /
likes / dislikes this", "Nick reviewed this", shelf *Reviewed*, control
*Review*, modal heading and placeholder per the writing-copy skill at
implementation. Settings: "A review is always shared — writing one is the
act of sharing." Status: "activity traffic". Feed empty state: "What your
friends review and want to watch lands here".

### Scope cost

* A wire change: the protocol page, a relay release (v0.6.0, the owner
  tags and deploys), a schema and a data migration, and a rename that
  touches ~40 modules, ~15 test files, 8 stories, 10 wiki pages and 2
  records. Four to five sessions.
* Every recommendation ever made disappears on upgrade — on this install
  9 of the owner's own (6 with words) and 2 from friends — and people
  review again. The alternative, re-signing own rows as reviews on the
  first boot after the upgrade, needs the identity secret at runtime, so
  it is a one-shot path inside the app rather than a migration: a
  fallback the house rules forbid, for nine rows. Recommended against;
  the nine can be printed for hand re-entry before the upgrade.
* Installs on the old version publish 32160 events the new version drops
  and cannot read 32164 until they update — the same window ADR-067
  accepted for 32162.

## Decisions made

* `2026-09-12` — Owner's direction: "recommend" becomes "review"; a
  review has an optional text and an optional sentiment of thumbs down,
  thumbs up or heart, none of it required; the review is a new social
  event that replaces the recommendation entirely, vocabulary included.
* `2026-09-12` — A review is a new kind, 32164, and 32160 is retired,
  because an absent sentiment changes meaning (like → none) and the
  message's claim changes (advice → opinion); the protocol's own rule for
  a change of meaning applies, and ADR-067 is the precedent for how a kind
  retires. Relay v0.6.0 must accept 32164 before the app ships it.
* `2026-09-12` — The sentiment keeps its name and gains `dislike`; its
  absence is a real state, nil on the row, nothing on the wire. "Sentiment"
  is established for polarity with intensity; "rating" would imply a
  scale and "reaction" an emoji.
* `2026-09-12` — A review with neither sentiment nor text is valid, as the
  owner asked. It reads "Nick reviewed · Sample Movie" on the Feed and
  "Nick reviewed this" on the mast.
* `2026-09-12` — One sentiment glyph component for every surface;
  nothing-for-Like ends. The 500-character cap stays. The control's glyph
  becomes the pencil-square, named Review.
* `2026-09-12` — **The stored recommendations are removed** by the data
  migration, as ADR-067 removed tracking rows; no re-signing path. The
  owner approved the design and this disposition together, and the nine
  own recommendations were printed for hand re-entry (owner's direction).
